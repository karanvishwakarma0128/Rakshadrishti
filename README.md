# 🚨 RakshaDrishti AI

### AI-Powered Disaster Detection, Analysis & Rescue Coordination Platform

> **Detect • Analyze • Alert • Save Lives**

RakshaDrishti AI is an AI-powered disaster response platform that combines Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Geospatial Intelligence to enable faster, smarter, and more coordinated emergency response.

The platform is designed to help emergency teams detect survivors, assess damage severity, monitor disaster zones, identify safer rescue routes, locate nearby hospitals and shelters, and coordinate rescue operations through a centralized dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Current disaster response operations are often delayed due to the lack of real-time situational awareness.

During disasters such as floods, earthquakes, landslides, and wildfires, emergency teams face challenges in:

- Quickly locating survivors
- Assessing the severity of damage
- Identifying hazardous areas
- Planning safe rescue routes
- Coordinating rescue teams
- Allocating emergency resources efficiently
- Sharing critical information in real time

These delays can increase response time, cause inefficient resource allocation, and ultimately put more lives at risk.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**RakshaDrishti AI** transforms disaster response from a reactive process into a proactive, data-driven system.

The platform uses AI, Computer Vision, and geospatial intelligence to analyze disaster-related information and provide actionable insights to emergency responders.

### Core Capabilities

- 🤖 AI-based Survivor Detection
- 🏚️ AI Damage Severity Assessment
- 🗺️ Live Disaster Monitoring Map
- 🚑 Smart Rescue Route Planning
- 🏥 Hospital & Shelter Locator
- 🚨 Priority Alert Generation
- 👥 Rescue Team Coordination
- 📊 Centralized Command & Control Dashboard

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🤖 AI Survivor Detection

Detects potential survivors from disaster-related images and visual data using computer vision techniques.

The system can work with data sources such as:

- Drone imagery
- CCTV cameras
- Satellite imagery
- Mobile reports

Detected survivor locations can be shared with rescue teams for faster response.

---

### 2. 🏚️ AI Damage Severity Assessment

The system analyzes affected areas and generates a **Damage Severity Score from 0–100**.

| Score | Severity | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 0–30 | Minor | Low damage |
| 🟡 31–70 | Moderate | Significant damage |
| 🔴 71–100 | Severe | Critical damage |

This allows emergency teams to prioritize the most critical areas.

---

### 3. 🗺️ Live Disaster Monitoring Map

The centralized map provides an overview of the disaster situation, including:

- Disaster zones
- Survivor locations
- Damage severity
- Rescue team positions
- Emergency locations

This helps responders maintain real-time situational awareness.

---

### 4. 🚑 Smart Rescue Route Planning

The platform assists rescue teams in selecting safer and more efficient routes by considering disaster zones and hazardous areas.

This helps reduce unnecessary travel time and improves rescue efficiency.

---

### 5. 🏥 Hospital & Shelter Locator

The system helps rescue teams identify nearby:

- Hospitals
- Medical facilities
- Shelters
- Safe locations

This enables faster medical assistance and evacuation planning.

---

### 6. 🚨 Priority Alert Generation

The platform can generate priority alerts based on:

- Damage severity
- Survivor locations
- Disaster intensity
- Hazard levels
- Emergency requirements

Critical situations can therefore receive immediate attention.

---

### 7. 👥 Rescue Team Coordination

A centralized system allows emergency coordinators to monitor:

- Rescue teams
- Team locations
- Survivor locations
- Disaster zones
- Emergency alerts
- Resource requirements

---

### 8. 📊 Centralized Command Dashboard

The dashboard brings all important information into one place.

It provides:

- Live disaster monitoring
- Survivor locations
- Damage severity view
- Rescue team tracking
- Alerts and notifications
- Reports and analytics

# 🔄 How It Works

## 📥 Step 1 — Data Collection

Disaster-related information is collected from available sources such as images, sensors, reports, and location data.

## ⚙️ Step 2 — Data Processing

The collected data is cleaned, processed, and prepared for analysis.

## 🧠 Step 3 — AI Analysis

AI and computer vision modules analyze the incoming data to identify:

- Potential survivors
- Damaged structures
- Hazardous zones
- Disaster severity

## ⚠️ Step 4 — Risk Assessment

The system evaluates the severity and priority of different disaster-affected areas.

## 🎯 Step 5 — Decision Support

The platform provides useful recommendations such as:

- Priority rescue locations
- Safer rescue routes
- Nearby hospitals
- Nearby shelters
- Resource allocation

## 📊 Step 6 — Centralized Monitoring

All critical information is displayed on the command and control dashboard.

## 🚑 Step 7 — Rescue Response

Emergency teams can use these insights to make faster and more informed decisions.
